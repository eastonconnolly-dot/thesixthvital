Outcomes

What changes after two days

01

A shared vocabulary

Every participant scores encounters the same way afterward — Read, Mode, Delivery, Adaptation, Outcome — so feedback stops being a matter of opinion.

02

A method that survives the shift

Real encounters don't stay in one mode. Participants drill the moment a patient flips from wanting proof to wanting permission — and catch it.

03

A measurable lift

Baseline and final encounters are scored on the same 25-point rubric, so the improvement is a number, not an impression.

Built for three tracks

Applicants headed into interviews. Physicians and surgeons in practice. Hospitals, practices, and residency/fellowship programs training a cohort at once.
