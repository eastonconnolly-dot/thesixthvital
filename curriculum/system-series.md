# System Series — $90,000

**Track:** Program (hospital or health system) · **Format:** multi-session series across a full academic year (~9–10 months) · **Price:** request proposal

Not a single event — a syllabus. Built from the same blocks as the other
program scripts, spaced across a year so the method compounds instead of
fading after one intensive. Adjust exact months to the program's own
academic calendar (this assumes a roughly August–May year); the shape
matters more than the exact dates.

## The year, at a glance

| When | Session | Builds on |
|---|---|---|
| Month 1 | Kickoff — full 2-day cohort intensive | `program-cohort-2day.md`, run in full |
| Month 3 | Half-day booster — Shift refresher | New scenario variety, no re-teaching basics |
| Month 5 | Half-day booster — compound high-stakes room | Multiple family members + mid-encounter shifts together |
| Month 7 | Mid-year check-in — re-score + leadership debrief | Filmed re-encounter vs. original baseline |
| Month 9 | Closing intensive — final filmed encounters | Full cohort re-score |
| Month 9–10 | Year-end leadership debrief | Year-over-year outcomes report |

## Month 1 — Kickoff

Run `program-cohort-2day.md` exactly as written, including its leadership
debrief at the end. This is the foundation the rest of the year builds on
— don't compress it because there's more time coming later; the full
method needs to land completely here.

## Month 3 — Half-day booster: Shift refresher

3.5–4 hours. No re-teaching Read/Pick/Speak/Ask — assume retention from
the kickoff and confirm it with one quick integrated encounter per person instead
of a full teaching block. Spend the bulk of the time on fresh
`shift_to_mode` scenarios the cohort hasn't seen yet, mixing all four
physician scenarios. Close with a short group discussion: where has the
method actually shown up in real work since the kickoff, and where hasn't
it stuck yet.

## Month 5 — Half-day booster: compound high-stakes room

3.5–4 hours. This is where the difficulty steps up past anything in the
kickoff: small groups of 3–4, multiple family members with different
P-modes *and* a mid-scenario shift, run together rather than as separate
skills. This is the module that actually separates a one-time intensive
from a real year-long program — nobody gets this encounter volume from a single
event.

## Month 7 — Mid-year check-in

Half day. Each participant gets a short filmed re-encounter, scored
against their *original* Month 1 baseline, not against Month 5 — the point
is to show movement across the whole year, not just since the last
booster. Close with a leadership debrief on the aggregate mid-year numbers,
same format as the kickoff's closing debrief: patterns, not individuals,
and what the back half of the year should focus on based on where the
cohort is still clustering low.

## Month 9 — Closing intensive

Full day. Final filmed encounters for the full cohort, same peer-drilling
structure as `program-cohort-1day.md`'s afternoon block for a last round of
encounters beforehand if the group wants the warm-up, then straight to filming
and scoring.

## Month 9–10 — Year-end leadership debrief

With program leadership. Walk the full year's numbers — Month 1 baseline
against Month 9 final, with the Month 7 mid-year point plotted between
them so the trend is visible, not just the endpoints. This is the
renewal conversation: what a second year would specifically address, based
on real data from this one, not a generic pitch.
