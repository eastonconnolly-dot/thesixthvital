# Program Cohort — 2 Day

**Track:** Program (hospitals, practices, residency/fellowship programs) · **Format:** 2 days, on-site, plus a leadership debrief · **Price:** request proposal

Day 1 runs identically to `program-cohort-1day.md` through the paired
Speak drilling block — don't rewrite it, just follow that script through
2:15pm on Day 1. What's different is everything after that: more rep
volume, a proper Shift block instead of a compressed one, and a leadership
debrief closing out Day 2. Same scaling notes apply for larger groups.

## Pre-session checklist

Same as the 1-Day version, plus: confirm who from program leadership
(program director, chief residents) will attend the Day 2 closing debrief,
and get that on their calendar before day one — don't try to schedule it
day-of.

---

## Day 1 — Foundations (through Speak)

Follow `program-cohort-1day.md` exactly through its 2:15–2:30 break. Stop
there — Day 1 ends after the Speak drilling block, not at 4:30. Extra time
freed up by not compressing Ask/Shift into Day 1 goes into a second, deeper
paired-drilling round on Read + Pick + Speak before the day closes.

**2:30–3:15 — Extended paired drilling: Read + Pick + Speak**
A second full round, new cards, more reps per person than the 1-Day
version allows.

**3:15–3:30 — Break**

**3:30–4:00 — Teach: Ask**
Full module, not compressed with Shift this time — Acknowledge, Stop, Know
what they want.

**4:00–4:30 — Day 1 close**
Preview Day 2: full Shift module, a group high-stakes-room exercise, more
filmed reps, and the leadership debrief at the end.

---

## Day 2 — Shift, high-stakes room, final encounters, leadership debrief

**9:00–9:15 — Recap**
Quick group warm-up, no scoring.

**9:15–10:00 — Teach: Shift**
Full module — See, Hold, Identify, Flip, Test.

**10:00–11:00 — Paired drilling: Shift-integrated**
Full reps with unannounced mid-rep P-mode switches.

**11:00–11:15 — Break**

**11:15–12:15 — Group high-stakes room**
Split into small groups of 3–4; one person is the physician, the others
play family members with different P-modes simultaneously, cards define
who wants what. Rotate roles so everyone gets a turn as the physician at
least once. This is the group-format version of Physician Private's
family-conference exercise.

**12:15–1:15 — Lunch**

**1:15–2:15 — Filmed final reps**
Higher rep volume than the 1-Day version — this is what the extra day
buys. One at a time for small groups, parallel clusters for larger ones.

**2:15–2:30 — Break**

**2:30–3:00 — Individual scorecard review**
Brief 1:1 windows or small-cluster review — same "don't read scores to
the group" rule as the 1-Day version.

**3:00–4:00 — Leadership debrief**
With the program director and/or chief residents who confirmed attendance
during pre-session prep. Walk through the cohort-level score report — the
same aggregate data that goes into the cohort scorecard PDF and, for
program-track deals, the leadership-deck version of it (see
`services/delivery.py`'s cohort email logic). Frame it around patterns,
not individuals: where did the group cluster low, which P-mode caused the
most trouble, what would a follow-up engagement address. This is the
moment that turns one cohort into a renewal or a referral to another
program.
