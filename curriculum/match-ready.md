# Match Ready — $10,000

**Track:** Applicant · **Format:** 2-day private intensive, one applicant, filmed baseline and final encounter · **Guarantee:** 6-point rubric lift, baseline to final, or continue working at no charge until it hits.

Uses the `mmi_station` and `panel_question` scenarios and the four P-modes
(Proof, Plan, Permission, Power) from `shared/prompts/scenarios.py`. Every
taught segment below pulls from the matching lesson in
`shared/prompts/lessons.py` — read those once before your first real
session; the summaries here are cues for what to say live, not the full text.

## Pre-session checklist

- Pull the qualifier brief and application `situation` field before day one
  — you should already know their target programs, interview timeline, and
  whatever they flagged as their own weak spot. Reference it out loud in the
  welcome; it's the fastest trust-builder you have.
- Filming setup ready and tested (see `curriculum/README.md`).
- Printed rubric card, two copies (baseline + final).
- Blank one-page playbook template for the Day 2 close.
- Confirm room setup: two chairs facing each other, nothing between you —
  the physical setup matters for the Speak module (Sit).

## Day 1 — Read, Pick the P, Speak

**9:00–9:20 — Welcome (not filmed)**
Rapport first. Confirm their timeline and target programs out loud from the
brief. Set expectations: "Today we build the four pieces. Tomorrow we add
the fifth — the one that actually separates good from great — and film
where you land." Show them the badge and explain the scorecard now, so they
know what they're working toward.

**9:20–9:45 — Filmed baseline encounter**
One `mmi_station` rep, cold. No coaching, no warmup beyond the welcome. Pick
whichever P-mode the brief suggests will be hardest for them; default to
Proof if you're not sure. This is the "before" tape — let it run its
natural length, don't rescue them if it goes sideways.

**9:45–10:15 — Score, reveal, don't unpack yet**
Score the rubric live in front of them. Show the number. Say what it means
in one sentence — "this is where you start, not where you're stuck" — and
move on. Don't explain what went wrong yet; the gap is the motivation for
the rest of the day.

**10:15–10:30 — Break**

**10:30–11:15 — Teach: Read**
Room, Emotion, Angle, Desire. Use their own baseline tape as the example —
pause it at the moment they missed something and ask "what was actually in
the room right there?" before you tell them.

**11:15–12:00 — Drill: Read only**
3–4 rapid `mmi_station` reps. After each: stop before they respond in-scene
and ask "what did you just read — Room, Emotion, Angle, Desire?" Don't let
them proceed until they've named all four, even as a guess. No scoring yet.

**12:00–1:00 — Lunch**

**1:00–1:45 — Teach: Pick the P**
Proof / Plan / Permission / Power, reframed for an interview panel: a Proof
interviewer wants specifics on what you actually did; Plan wants to hear
your next steps if admitted; Permission is watching whether you can be
directed, not just impressive; Power is testing how you handle being
challenged. Use the P-mode descriptions in `scenarios.py` verbatim where
useful — they're written to be said out loud.

**1:45–2:45 — Drill: Pick the P + respond**
4 reps, one per P-mode, `panel_question` scenario. Debrief after each in one
line: "which P did you read, and did your answer actually match it?"

**2:45–3:00 — Break**

**3:00–3:45 — Teach: Speak**
Sit, Pace, Eyes, Air, Kill the fillers. Run an isolation drill: same simple
question, four reps, one dial per rep — this one is Pace only, next is Eyes
only. Isolating the dial is what makes it correctable; bundled together
it's just "be more confident," which isn't a real instruction.

**3:45–4:30 — Drill: Read + Pick + Speak, integrated**
3–4 full reps, no Ask or Shift yet. This is the first time everything so
far runs together.

**4:30–4:50 — Day 1 debrief**
Name their single weakest dial from today, specifically — not "keep working
on delivery," the actual dial (e.g. "you drop eye contact every time you
mention your gap year"). Preview Day 2 in one sentence: "tomorrow the panel
is allowed to change on you mid-answer, and we film where you land."

**4:50–5:00 — Wrap**
Log the baseline half of the Scorecard in the system now, while it's fresh.

## Day 2 — Ask, Shift, mock panel, final encounter

**9:00–9:15 — Recap + warm-up rep**
One quick `mmi_station` rep, no scoring, just to reset the muscle from
yesterday.

**9:15–10:00 — Teach: Ask**
Acknowledge, Stop, Know what they want. For a panel context: acknowledge a
hard question landed as hard ("that's a fair challenge on my application"),
stop fully instead of rushing to fill the silence, then close with
something that confirms you actually addressed what they were probing for
— not "does that make sense," an actual question back when the format
allows it.

**10:00–11:00 — Drill: Ask integrated**
3–4 full reps, Read + Pick + Speak + Ask, `mmi_station`.

**11:00–11:15 — Break**

**11:15–12:00 — Teach: Shift**
See, Hold, Identify, Flip, Test. Explain that today's panel is allowed to
change what it wants mid-answer without announcing it — same as a real
panel that starts skeptical and warms up, or starts friendly and turns
adversarial. This is the module every other interview-prep service skips.

**12:00–1:00 — Lunch**

**1:00–2:30 — Drill: Shift-integrated reps**
4–5 reps using the `shift_to_mode` mechanic — pick a starting P-mode, let
it flip partway through, don't announce it. Mix `mmi_station` and
`panel_question`. Debrief each: "where did it shift, and how fast did you
catch it?" — score recovery speed, not whether they caught it instantly.

**2:30–2:45 — Break**

**2:45–3:15 — Mock panel (3 stations back-to-back)**
Run three short stations back-to-back, playing a different interviewer
persona for each (skeptical Proof, rushed Plan, testing Power works well as
a set) — no debrief between stations, just like a real MMI circuit. If you
have a colleague available to guest as a second interviewer for this block,
it scales up naturally; solo works fine too.

**3:15–3:45 — Filmed final encounter**
One `mmi_station` or `panel_question` rep, same difficulty as the baseline,
unprompted. This is the "after" tape.

**3:45–4:15 — Score final, compare live**
Fill in the final Scorecard fields with them watching. Reveal the lift
together — this is the guarantee checkpoint. If it's under 6 points, say so
plainly and schedule the continuation session right there before they
leave; don't let it become an awkward follow-up email later.

**4:15–4:40 — Personal playbook**
Write it with them, one page: their weakest dial, their most common P-mode
blind spot, one specific thing to drill before their real interview. This
is what they actually keep and reread.

**4:40–5:00 — Close**
Explain what arrives next — badge, scorecard PDF, clip library, 90 days of
Practice app access to keep drilling. If the guarantee is in play, confirm
the follow-up date before they walk out the door.
