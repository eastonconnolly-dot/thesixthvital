# Applicant Cohort Seat — $1,800/seat

**Track:** Applicant · **Format:** 1-day scheduled cohort (aim for 6–10 seats), filmed individual baseline and final · **Note:** this is peer-practice, not 1:1 — the group drills on each other while you teach and circulate.

Peer pairs use printed scenario cards instead of the AI simulator — hand
each pair a card with the `setup` text from `mmi_station` or
`panel_question` in `shared/prompts/scenarios.py`, plus which P-mode to
play. The "interviewer" partner reads it and stays in character; that's
the whole prep they need.

## Pre-session checklist

- Printed scenario cards, enough for every pair to have 3–4 different ones
  through the day (mix MMI Station and Panel Interview Question, cover all
  four P-modes across the set).
- One rubric card per participant.
- Name tags or a seating chart if the cohort doesn't already know each
  other — pairing works better when you can call people by name fast.

---

## Run-of-show (1 day, ~6–10 seats)

**9:00–9:20 — Group welcome**
Introduce the day, the method, and the format: everyone gets filmed twice
(a short baseline and a short final), and spends the rest of the day
drilling on each other while you circulate.

**9:20–10:15 — Individual filmed baseline reps**
One at a time, in front of the group, ~5 minutes each including the
scenario: applicant plays themselves, you play the interviewer. Everyone
else watches and takes one note — what did this person miss that they'd
have caught watching someone else's? Watching is Read practice too.

**10:15–10:30 — Break**

**10:30–11:15 — Teach: Read + Pick the P**
To the whole group at once, same content as Match Ready's Day 1 modules,
compressed into one block.

**11:15–12:00 — Paired drilling: Read + Pick the P**
Split into pairs. Hand out scenario cards. Each person runs 2 reps as the
applicant, 2 as the interviewer (playing the card's P-mode). Circulate —
you're not running every rep, you're spot-coaching across the room.

**12:00–1:00 — Lunch**

**1:00–1:30 — Teach: Speak**
Compressed version of Match Ready's Speak module — the five dials, one
group demonstration per dial rather than individual isolation drills.

**1:30–2:15 — Paired drilling: Speak focus**
Same pairs, new cards. This round, the observing partner's job is
specifically to call out filler words and broken eye contact after each
rep — peer feedback on the mechanical dials, since that's the easiest
thing for a non-expert partner to catch accurately.

**2:15–2:30 — Break**

**2:30–3:00 — Teach: Ask + Shift (combined)**
Compressed — cohort format doesn't have room for two separate modules
here. Explain both, then explain that the next drilling round includes
unannounced shifts.

**3:00–3:30 — Paired drilling: full integration with shifts**
New cards, some marked to shift mid-rep (partner switches P-mode partway
through without announcing it, per the card).

**3:30–4:15 — Individual filmed final reps**
Same format as the morning baseline, one at a time in front of the group.

**4:15–4:30 — Close**
Group thank-you and a one-line explanation of what arrives next
(scorecard, badge). Individual score comparisons happen 1:1, briefly, as
people collect their things — don't read anyone's number out loud to the
group.
